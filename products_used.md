# Products I Actually Use

**This file is the evidence gate for Variant A emails.** See
`docs/EMAIL-TEMPLATE.md` §3.

The drafter may only write "I've been using your product and here's what I
found" if the company appears below with **at least two dated entries**. If a
company isn't here, the email falls back to Variant B automatically.

## Why this file exists

An LLM asked to produce "three suggestions for their product" will invent three
plausible ones. A founder reads their own product every day and spots a
fabricated bug immediately — it's worse than sending a generic email, because
it proves you'll say things you haven't verified.

You cannot fake having used something. So: only what you've actually used, only
what you actually noticed.

## How to add an entry

Use the product, notice something, write it down the same day. Two minutes.

Good entries are specific and reproducible. Bad ones are vague or are really
just feature wishes dressed up as observations.

- Good: *"Bulk CSV import silently drops rows where the email column has a
  trailing space. Lost 12 of 400 with no warning."*
- Bad: *"The import flow could be better."*

---

## Template

```
### Company Name
- **Product:** what it is
- **Used since:** YYYY-MM, roughly how much
- **Observations:**
  - `YYYY-MM-DD` — what you hit, specific enough that they could reproduce it
  - `YYYY-MM-DD` — a UI friction point, where exactly
  - `YYYY-MM-DD` — a feature gap and the concrete situation you wanted it in
```
